## What is BRAINR?

BRAINR is a **flexible, enterprise-grade AI middleware platform** that bridges the gap between static legacy applications and the power of modern artificial intelligence. At its core, BRAINR excels at understanding natural language queries, converting them into actionable data operations, maintaining context across multiple conversation turns, and delivering responses in the user's native language.

### Core Capabilities

1. **Natural Language to Data Operations (NL-to-SQL)**: Users ask questions in plain English (or any language), and BRAINR intelligently converts them into complex database queries, API calls, or business logic executions.

2. **Multi-Turn Intelligent Conversations**: Unlike one-off AI chatbots, BRAINR maintains full conversation context. Follow-up questions like "break that down by region" or "show me only the high-value ones" are interpreted with full awareness of previous exchanges.

3. **Flexible AI Backbone**: Plug in OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or run offline with free open-source models. Switch providers in seconds. No vendor lock-in.

4. **Microservice Architecture**: Deploy as a lightweight microservice that integrates with your existing authentication, database, and application layers—all in minutes.

5. **Multi-Language & Accessibility**: Automatic translation, speech-to-text in African languages, and text-to-speech—breaking down communication barriers.

6. **Customizable AI Context**: Admins can configure exactly how AI should behave, what rules it should follow, and what business logic it should understand—all through an intuitive interface.

7. **Audit & Compliance**: Every AI interaction is logged, tracked, and auditable for compliance, security, and operational insight.
